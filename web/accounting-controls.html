Financial Controls

Trial Balance · General Ledger · GST Reconciliation · Period Close

Control rule

Accounting periods can be closed only when the posting ledger is balanced. Reports are sourced from posted accounting transactions and tax transaction lines.

Endpoints
  • GET /v90cb/accounting/trial-balance
  • GET /v90cb/accounting/general-ledger
  • GET /v90cb/accounting/gst-reconciliation
  • POST /v90cb/accounting/periods/{period_id}/close
