Working Capital, Cash & Credit Cockpit

Evidence-based management snapshots covering liquidity, receivables, payables, inventory, credit utilization, collections and cash-conversion cycle.

Core KPIs

  • Cash available and payment commitments
  • Receivables and overdue receivables
  • Payables and overdue payables
  • Inventory value and inventory days
  • Credit limit, utilization and collection realization
  • DSO, DPO and Cash Conversion Cycle
  • Net Working Capital = Receivables + Inventory − Payables
