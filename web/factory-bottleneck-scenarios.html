Factory Bottleneck Scenarios

Simulate overtime, labour reallocation, schedule reduction and OEE improvement against the current factory bottleneck baseline.

Operational APIs

POST /v90ed/factory-bottleneck/scenarios/simulate
GET /v90ed/factory-bottleneck/scenarios
GET /v90ed/factory-bottleneck/scenarios/{scenario_id}
POST /v90ed/factory-bottleneck/scenarios/periods/close

Scenario semantics

Overtime increases labour availability.
Labour reallocation reduces required hours.
Schedule reduction reduces machine load.
OEE gain improves scenario OEE.
