Predictive Maintenance Risk

Risk-ranked early-warning view from observed breakdown, repeat-failure and downtime history. Advisory only; it does not automatically change maintenance plans.

Workflow
  1. Calculate a monthly risk snapshot for each work centre.
  2. Review critical/high-risk assets or work centres.
  3. Use the recommended action to prioritize inspection or PM review.
  4. Close the period after management review.
Risk levels

Critical ≥ 70 · High ≥ 40 · Medium ≥ 20 · Low < 20. Scores are operational indicators, not a machine-learning failure probability.
