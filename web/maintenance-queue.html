Maintenance Priority Queue

Risk-weighted advisory queue combining predictive maintenance risk, breakdown downtime, order volume and maintenance cost. It does not automatically create or reschedule work orders.

Priority logic
  • 60% predictive risk score
  • 20% breakdown-hour exposure
  • 15% maintenance-cost exposure
  • 5% breakdown-order frequency
Response targets
  • Critical — immediate inspection
  • High — within 24 hours
  • Medium — within 48 hours
  • Low — within 72 hours / monitor
