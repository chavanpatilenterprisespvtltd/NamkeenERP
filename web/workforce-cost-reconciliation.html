Workforce–Payroll–Production Cost Reconciliation

Reconcile payroll employer cost, allocated labour, production labour and labour budget for a period.

Dashboard

Pass organization_id, entity_id and optional period_id in the URL.

Operational APIs

POST /v90dx/workforce/cost-reconciliation
GET /v90dx/workforce/cost-reconciliation
POST /v90dx/workforce/cost-reconciliation/{period_id}/close
GET /v90dx/workforce/cost-reconciliation/dashboard
