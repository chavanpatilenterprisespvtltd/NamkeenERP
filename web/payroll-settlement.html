Payroll Banking, Payslips & Statutory Settlement

Settlement Flow

Bank Mapping → Payment Batch → Approval → Payment Status → Payslip → Statutory Settlement → Reconciliation

Controls

  • Employee bank details are masked in storage/display.
  • Payment batches require approval before settlement.
  • Failed payments retain a failure reason and can be reprocessed.
  • Payroll-to-bank reconciliation records matched and exception states.
  • Statutory settlement references remain auditable.
