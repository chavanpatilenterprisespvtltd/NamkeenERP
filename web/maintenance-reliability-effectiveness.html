Reliability Executive Action Effectiveness

Controlled post-action benefit review. Completed executive actions require explicit effectiveness assessment and evidence before period closure. This module does not claim causal attribution and does not mutate operations automatically.
ControlPurpose
CompletionTrack approved and completed executive actions.
Benefit reviewAssess completed actions as effective, ineffective, or not assessed with evidence.
Executive scoreCombine completion, review coverage, effectiveness and existing benefit evidence into a transparent 0–100 score.
CloseBlock period closure when completed actions remain unassessed unless explicitly forced.
